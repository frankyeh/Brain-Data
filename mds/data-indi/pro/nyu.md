### **NYU Institute for Pediatric Neuroscience (IPN) Sample**

---

### **Project Overview**

The **NYU Phyllis Green and Randolph Cōwen Institute for Pediatric Neuroscience (IPN)** sample is a curated collection of neuroimaging data from **psychiatrically screened individuals aged 6 to 55 years**.

The initial release includes datasets from **49 psychiatrically neurotypical adults**, accompanied by age, gender, and intelligence quotient (IQ) data. Future releases will expand to include child and adolescent datasets, comprehensive phenotypic information, and individuals from clinical populations.

---

### **Available Data for Each Participant**

* At least one **6-minute resting-state fMRI scan (R-fMRI)**

  * *Most participants have two R-fMRI scans, collected less than one hour apart during the same session. Rest\_1 is always collected first.*
* One **high-resolution T1-weighted MPRAGE anatomical scan** (defaced to protect confidentiality)
* Two **64-direction DTI scans**
* **Demographic data:** Age and gender
* **IQ Measures:** Verbal, Performance, and Composite scores (via the **Weschler Abbreviated Scale of Intelligence \[WASI]**)

---

### **Important Notice**

Some participants in this release also appear in the **NewYork\_a** dataset from the 1000 Functional Connectomes classic collection and the **NYU\_Cocaine** dataset from the INDI retrospective release. **Do not combine this dataset with the NewYork\_a or NYU\_Cocaine datasets** to avoid duplication errors.

### **Data Access**

To access the dataset:

1. Register for a free account on [NITRC](https://www.nitrc.org/).
2. Once registered, [request access to the 1000 Functional Connectomes Project](https://www.nitrc.org/projects/fcon_1000/).
3. Approval is typically granted within one business day.

**Available Downloads:**
