### **Beijing Normal University (Short TR Sample)**

---

### **Dataset Overview**

This dataset was collected at the **State Key Laboratory of Cognitive Neuroscience and Learning, Beijing Normal University**. It contains neuroimaging and demographic data from **healthy college-aged volunteers**.

**For each participant, the following data is included:**

* Resting-state fMRI scans with a **TR of 2.0 seconds** (240 repetitions)
* Resting-state fMRI scans with a **short TR of 0.4 seconds** (1200 repetitions)
* A **defaced MPRAGE anatomical scan**
* A **64-direction diffusion tensor imaging (DTI) scan** (2 mm isotropic resolution)
* **Demographic information**

The dataset was released incrementally, with **3 new subjects added each week**.
