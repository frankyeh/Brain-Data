### **Nathan Kline Institute (NKI) / Rockland Sample**

---

### **Project Overview**

The **NKI/Rockland Sample** is a **phenotypically rich neuroimaging dataset** collected from individuals aged **4 to 85 years**. Participants undergo semi-structured diagnostic psychiatric interviews and complete a comprehensive battery of psychiatric, cognitive, and behavioral assessments, providing extensive phenotypic data to support research into brain–behavior relationships.

The primary goal of this initiative is to generate a **large-scale, deeply phenotyped dataset** to accelerate discovery science in neuroscience and mental health. To support this, the dataset is shared **prospectively during the course of acquisition**, typically with a randomized 2–8 week release lag.

---

### **Available Data Per Participant**

* **10-minute resting-state fMRI scan (R-fMRI)** *(Adapted from Brain Genomics Superstruct Project)*
* **6-direction diffusion tensor imaging (DTI) scan** *(Adapted from Brain Genomics Superstruct Project)*
* **64-direction DTI scan** (2 mm isotropic resolution)
* **MPRAGE anatomical scan**
* **MPRAGE anatomical scan (Shorter sequence)**
* **T2-weighted anatomical sequence**
* **Comprehensive psychiatric, cognitive, and behavioral assessments**

Data for **5–10 participants per week** is distributed as it becomes available.

---

### **Data Access and Disclaimer**

Data is shared **prospectively**, and as such, is subject to challenges typical of ongoing imaging studies, including potential data losses or equipment issues. Any dataset that does not meet internal quality control standards will be flagged accordingly but still released to maintain dataset completeness.

Data is shared under a **Creative Commons Attribution – Non-Commercial License**.

For the latest data releases and access, visit the [NKI/Rockland Sample Project Page](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/pro/nki.html).
