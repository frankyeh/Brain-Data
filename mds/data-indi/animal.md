# PRIMatE Data Exchange (PRIME-DE)

The **PRIMatE Data Exchange (PRIME-DE)** (`data-indi/animal`) is an open science initiative for **non-human primate (NHP) neuroimaging** that aggregates and shares multi-site MRI datasets from laboratories worldwide.

- **Original portal:** [INDI PRIME-DE](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/indiPRIME.html)
- **Quality control & search:** [QC results](/qc.html) · [Search datasets using the QC report](/qc_search.html)
- **Download instructions:** [Download guide](/download.html) (file formats, DSI Studio GUI, CLI, and Python)
- **Secondary direct assets:** [GitHub Releases ↗](https://github.com/data-indi/animal/releases)

## Overview

PRIME-DE provides a platform for sharing functional, diffusion, and structural MRI data to support in vivo studies of non-human primate brain organization, structural and functional networks, and cross-species comparisons.

### Modalities & Collections

Depending on the contributing site, datasets in PRIME-DE include:

- **Modalities:** Structural MRI (T1-weighted, T2-weighted), diffusion MRI, resting-state fMRI, task-based fMRI, naturalistic viewing fMRI, field maps, and magnetic resonance spectroscopy.
- **Collections:** Multiple macaque species (*Macaca mulatta*, *Macaca fascicularis*), awake and anesthetized protocols, and scanners spanning 1.5T, 3T, 7T, and beyond.
- **Quality assurance:** Evaluated with standardized quality metrics, including the Preprocessed Connectome Project Quality Assurance Protocol (QAP), alongside Fiber Data Hub `qc.tsv` tables.

## Usage Agreement & Citation

Data sharing policies vary by dataset and are defined by the contributing investigators (commonly **CC BY-NC-SA**, **CC BY**, or dataset-specific Data Usage Agreements through the 1000 Functional Connectomes Project / INDI portal). Users must comply with the license associated with each dataset and cite both PRIME-DE and the relevant dataset-specific publications.
