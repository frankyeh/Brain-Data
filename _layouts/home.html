About the Hub

The Fiber Data Hub shares processed fiber data derived from diffusion MRI: fiber orientations, anisotropy, diffusivities and other metrics needed for tractography, without redistributing raw DWI.

These derivatives are typically 50–100× smaller than the original scans, so large public datasets can be searched, quality-checked and analyzed without repeating preprocessing.
